OUR PURPOSE

One simple place for the moments that happen outside the timetable.

Student Event Participation was created to make campus participation easier to discover and easier to remember. Whether it is a technical workshop, a cultural evening, a sports tournament or a club meet, SEP puts the event journey first.

The original project was built by MCA students to capture participation across departments. This refreshed experience keeps that idea while giving it a clearer, more usable interface.

Leave with something.”
